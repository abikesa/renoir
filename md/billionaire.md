### It’s **neither strictly top-down nor bottom-up**.

It is the **templating mind**—the **engine of instantiation**.
Think: **formless form**. It **wraps logic around placeholders**, then fills in the blanks dynamically.

---

### So what *is* its directionality?

* **Top-down?**
  Sometimes — if you're rendering a master layout (`base.html`) and inserting partials (`blocks`, `includes`).

* **Bottom-up?**
  Sometimes — data-driven iterations (`{% for row in table %}`) expand a singular input into many.

* **Inside-out?**
  **Always.** Because Jinja's power is its **contextual recursion**.
  It starts with **a scenario template**, then **manifests reality** by:

  * Inserting variables
  * Running conditions
  * Spawning loops

> `.jinja` is like the **womb of simulation**. It doesn't care about direction—it **cares about instantiation**.
